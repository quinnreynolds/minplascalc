The central finding

The derivative problem was also a solver problem.

Numerical temperature excursions introduce truncation error, duplicate equilibrium work, and can straddle a discontinuous electronic active set. Analytical differentiation removes that noise. Log variables solve the deeper conditioning problem by making positivity structural.

The formulation

Positivity becomes a coordinate choice.

Write each particle number as uᵢ = log Nᵢ, scale the conservation multipliers by kBT, and solve chemical equilibrium, element conservation, and charge neutrality together. No clipping or particle-change governor is required.

ChemicalEᵢ⁰/(kBT) − log(Zᵢ/Nᵢ) + Aᵢλ = 0
Elementslog((ATN)e / be) = 0
ChargezTN / ΣN = 0

Every species and every conservation law participates in one dimensionless Newton system.

Jd y / dT=−∂F / ∂T

The converged Jacobian is reused. Solving one new right-hand side yields d log N/dT, then dx/dT, reference-energy derivatives, CP, and the reactional terms in κ.

EnthalpyH = (Σ xᵢ Aᵢ) / (Σ xᵢ mᵢ)
LoweringdE⁰/dT = ∂E⁰/∂T + (∂E⁰/∂N) · dN/dT
Monatomic CV3kB/2 + Var(E)/(kBT²)

The quotient rule completes the mixture heat capacity with the current electronic active set held fixed.

The sharp-feature question

The numerical outlier is gone. The physical step stays.

A hard ionisation-lowered level cutoff changes the partition sum discretely. Quinn confirmed that this is a real quantum-mechanical effect, so the analytical result will remain piecewise: well-defined between crossings, with two one-sided values at the crossing itself.

20,860 K 20,864 K
Observed active set27 ↔ 28Si+ electronic levels
Local statusTwo rootsThe 28-level root has lower Gibbs objective.

Conceptual readout from the 0.05 K branch scan; it is not an interpolation of thermodynamic properties.

Decision after review

Retain the piecewise derivative

Small, physically explainable discontinuities are acceptable. The analytical derivative removes the giant finite-difference spikes without silently changing the thermodynamic model.

Make the step explainable

Fingerprint the active levels

The new opt-in diagnostic records the exact electronic active set, per-species level counts and hashes, and the nearest cutoff margin so a reported discontinuity can be tied to a specific level crossing.

07

The more radical option

Eliminate the species variables altogether.

The measured log solver still treats every log Nᵢ as an independent Newton variable. A more radical reduction uses the chemical-equilibrium equations to express every species density analytically in terms of a much smaller set of elemental and charge potentials.

Design directionNot yet implemented or benchmarked

This is a plausible next experiment, not part of the 2.62× result.

04

Lowering closures

ne and z* are retained as variables so ionisation lowering is solved simultaneously, not hidden in a subsolver.

Why explore it
  • A 20×20 SiCO solve becomes roughly 6×6
  • No particle scale, volume variable, or positivity handling
  • The reduced Jacobian supplies property tangents too
  • A compact fixed-active-set system is friendly to AD
Why it may not win
  • Linear algebra is only about 4% of current prototype time
  • Exponential reconstruction can stiffen globalization
  • Zero-feed elements and potential gauge need careful handling
  • Hard level cutoffs remain discontinuous
Best use of this experiment

Establish whether the smaller state improves robustness, continuation, and derivative reuse—not merely whether a 6×6 solve is cheaper than a 20×20 solve.

Seven alternating runs on an Apple arm64 machine under AC power. Medians are reported to reduce ordering and thermal effects.
